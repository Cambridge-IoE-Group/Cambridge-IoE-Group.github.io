Dr. Beyza Ezgi Örtlek completed her high school education at Ankara Fen Lisesi in Ankara, Turkey. She received double major degrees (dual full diploma), a B.S. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering and a B.S. in Molecular Biology and Genetics, from Koç University, Türkiye. She recently completed her Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Koç University under the supervision of Prof. Özgür Barış Akan. She is currently a Visiting Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Centre for neXt Communications (CXC) at the Department of Engineering, University of Cambridge, UK.
