Mr. Zhengyang Zhang joined the Centre for neXt Communications (CXC) Group as a PhD student in 2023. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Electronic Information Engineering from Beihang University, awarded in 2020, and a Master of Research degree in Connected Electronic and Photonic Systems from the University of Cambridge, completed in 2021.


<br>

<h3>Research Interests</h3>

- Space Communication
- Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)
- THz Communication

<br>

<h3>Publications</h3>

1. Z. Zhang, O. B. Akan, “Analysis of Terahertz Communication Under Dust Storm Conditions on Mars,” IEEE Communications Letters, vol. 29, no. 2, pp. 388–392, February 2025.
2. Z. Zhang, S. Liu, S. M. Goetz, and O. B. Akan, “Novel Improvement of Least Mean Square Adaptive Algorithm for Non-monotonic Systems,” Optics Express, 2024.
